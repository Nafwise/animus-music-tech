Feedback & testing

Controller capture

Help Animus Engine Pro support your controller. This page records what your controller sends - right here in your browser - and makes a small file you can attach to your feedback. Nothing is sent anywhere until you choose to.

Get ready

  • Use Chrome, Edge or Opera on a computer - they can talk to MIDI devices; Firefox and Safari can't.
  • Plug the controller in, then close Serato, rekordbox, Animus and any other DJ or MIDI software. Windows lets only one program use a controller at a time.

Connect

Your browser will ask to let this page use MIDI devices - allow it, including "system exclusive messages" (that's how the controller says what it is).

Ask it who it is

The MIDI standard has a "who are you?" question. If your controller answers, its maker and model are exactly what Animus needs to recognise it.

Record every control

Start recording, type what you're about to touch (for example Left deck PLAY), touch it, then press Next control (or Enter) and carry on. The more you cover, the better:

  • every button - and again while holding Shift;
  • every knob and fader, slowly from one end to the other;
  • the jog wheels: touch the top, then spin each way, both on top and on the outside;
  • the pads, in each pad mode; the crossfader; any switches on the front or back.

Nothing recorded yet.

Send it

Save the file to keep it, or attach it straight to your feedback. It holds your controller's name and the messages it sent, and your browser's name - nothing else from your computer.

Using Serato or rekordbox with it?

This page hears what your controller sends - it can't listen in on what Serato or rekordbox send back to it. Their mapping files fill that gap, so attach one on the feedback page if you have it:

  • rekordbox: click MIDI to open MIDI Learn, choose your controller, and Export its mapping (a .csv file).
  • Serato: custom MIDI mappings are saved as .xml files in your Music folder, under _Serato_\MIDI.
