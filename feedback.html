The button opens an email to animusmusictech@gmail.com in your own email app, with your answers written in. You see it before it goes, and nothing is sent from this page. What you send is used only to improve Animus and, if you ask, to talk to you about testing. It's never sold or passed on, and you can ask for it to be deleted at any time.
