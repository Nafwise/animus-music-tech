Privacy

Privacy policy

The short version: this site sets no cookies, runs no analytics or ads, and doesn't track you. Its fonts are on the site itself. Your browser talks to GitHub, which hosts the site, and to no one else unless you click a link.

Visiting the site: hosting by GitHub

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages, run by GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. St., San Francisco, CA 94107, USA. To deliver a page, GitHub's servers receive what every web server receives: your IP address, the page asked for, the time, and your browser's name and version. GitHub states that it logs visitors' IP addresses for security purposes. I don't receive or see those logs.

  • Legal basis: Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR - the legitimate interest in delivering this site and keeping it secure.
  • Transfer to the USA: GitHub states that it complies with the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, its UK Extension and the Swiss-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, and that it otherwise relies on the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.
  • More, including how long GitHub keeps it: GitHub's privacy statement.

The download button

The Animus Engine Pro page asks GitHub (api.github.com) for the newest release, so its download button points at the current version. That request also goes from your browser to GitHub, with the same details as above, on the same legal basis. The download itself comes from GitHub's servers too. Nothing about it is kept in your browser.

Fonts

The fonts (Inter and Orbitron) are served from this site. Your browser doesn't contact Google, or anyone else, for them.

Feedback

The feedback page sends nothing itself. Its button opens an email in your own email app with your answers written in, and you decide whether to send it. If the email needs a file (a controller capture, or long answers), the page saves one to your computer; it stays there unless you attach it.

If you send the email, I receive what you wrote, your email address and the name your email shows, and anything you attach. The mailbox is a Gmail account, so Google stores those emails for me.

  • Why, and the legal basis: to read and act on your feedback and reply to you - Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR, the legitimate interest in improving Animus and answering you. If you tick "You may email me about testing", also to contact you about testing - Art. 6 (1) (a) GDPR, your consent, which you can withdraw at any time, for example by replying "stop".
  • How long: as long as it takes to deal with your feedback, or the testing you signed up for. Then it's deleted. You can ask for that sooner at any time.
  • Do you have to? No. Everything is optional; without your email I just can't reply.

The controller capture

The capture page runs entirely in your browser. It uses Web MIDI to listen to your controller, after your browser asks your permission. Nothing is sent anywhere. If you press "Attach to my feedback", the recording is kept in your browser's session storage, for that tab only, so the feedback page can include it. It's deleted when you close the tab or press Remove. That storage is strictly necessary for the feature you asked for (§ 25 (2) no. 2 TDDDG), so it needs no consent. It's the only thing this site ever stores in your browser.

Links to other sites

The links to Instagram, Twitch and GitHub are plain links. Nothing loads from those sites, and they learn nothing about your visit here, unless you click one. Then their own privacy policies apply.

Your rights

Under the GDPR and the UK GDPR you can ask for access to your data (Art. 15), its correction (Art. 16), its deletion (Art. 17), restricted processing (Art. 18), and a copy to take elsewhere (Art. 20). You can object to processing based on legitimate interests (Art. 21), and withdraw consent at any time (Art. 7 (3)). Just email animusmusictech@gmail.com. There's no automated decision-making or profiling.

You can also complain to a data-protection authority: in the UK, the Information Commissioner's Office; in the EU, the authority where you live or work - in Germany, the data-protection authority of your federal state.
