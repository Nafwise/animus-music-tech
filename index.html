What it is

Pro tools shouldn't be a privilege

Animus Music Tech builds music software the way it should be: complete, running on your own PC, and free to use. Every feature unlocked from the first launch, no account to make, no monthly fee to keep paying.

It started as a home project - a DJ app built for my own sets - and grew because people got interested. It is now a family of three apps, made by one person and an AI pair-programmer.

The vision

Good tools, in the hands of the people who'd otherwise go without

Plenty of DJs and producers start out priced out of the software the pros use. Animus exists so that the fully unlocked version is simply the version. Money was never the end goal - getting a decent product into people's hands is.

01

A fully unlocked DJ app, right there when you need it

Four decks, stems separated on your own machine, proper FX, real controller support - and polished until it can stand next to the big names.

02

Mastering and a DAW that belong with it

Finish your mixes and your tracks in the same family of tools - one way of working, from the first idea to the master.

03

Your music stays yours

Local-first: tracks are analysed and split into stems on your PC, never uploaded. The internet is only used for updates, and to fetch the stem model the first time it's needed.

04

Built with the people using it

Testers shape what gets built next. Every bug report and every "could it do this?" is read. Join in.
